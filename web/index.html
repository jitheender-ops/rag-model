Chunking, vast

Eight strategies on one corpus, one embedder, one set of index params — fixed, recursive, sentence-window, semantic-drift, proposition, parent-document, metadata-filtered, multi-granularity. Scored against human relevance labels; s7 wins at 0.892 recall@50.

Under 200 ms

1500/1500 requests inside the window across warm, cold and concurrency-4. Enforced, not hoped for: a stage that cannot finish is never entered.

REQ 04

Latency analytics

P50 45.2 · P70 53.9 · P100 136.0 ms over 500 frozen queries × 3 modes, per stage, nearest-rank — never a single best-case run.

REQ 05

Harness

Six timed stages under one budget object, a degradation ladder, bounded deadlines on every unbounded call, structured JSON in and out, and one tool call the generator may make, capped at a single hop.

REQ 06

Guardrails

Four gates graded on 280 labelled queries: 84% correct abstention against 3.8% false, 100% injection resistance. It knows when not to answer — the last two examples above show it.
